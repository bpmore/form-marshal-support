# Form Marshal support

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests for **Form Marshal** and **Form Marshal Pro**, the WordPress plugins that find forms collecting health or personal information and block the ones that should not run.

This repository holds no code. It is the public place to ask for help.

## Before you post

**Everything here is public.** Do not post:

- patient information, form answers, or anything a person typed into a form
- your site's address, page names, or form names if they would identify your organization or your patients
- license keys, passwords, or API tokens
- security problems (see below)

Describe the form instead: "a text field labeled with a health question", "a Ninja Forms form with an email action", and so on. If we need details, we will ask you to send them privately.

## How to get help

- **[Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose)** and pick the template that fits: a bug, a question, or an idea.
- **Private matters** (anything about your organization, your account, or your license): use the email support listed with your Form Marshal Pro purchase.
- **Security problems:** report them privately through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](../../security/advisories/new). Please do not open a public issue.

## What helps us answer

- Form Marshal version, and Form Marshal Pro version if you use it
- WordPress and PHP versions, and whether the site is multisite
- The form plugin and its version (Gravity Forms, Ninja Forms, WPForms, or Contact Form 7)
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- Any error text, with private details removed

Form Marshal is a control, not a compliance guarantee. Support can explain what the plugin found and why; it cannot certify that a site meets HIPAA or any other rule.
